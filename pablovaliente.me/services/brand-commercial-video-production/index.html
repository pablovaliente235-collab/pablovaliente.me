Overview

In short

Commercial video has a job: to make someone stop, understand and act. Whether it is a product launch, a paid social campaign or a brand spot, the film has to look premium and work at the speed of a feed. I handle commercial video production in Spain from shoot to final grade, so the look stays consistent from the first frame to the last cutdown.

Working with one person who shoots, edits and colors means fewer hand-offs and faster decisions. I work across Spain, travel anywhere in Europe by arrangement, and can take on the post-production remotely for teams elsewhere.

How it works

From brief to delivery

  1. Brief

    We define the goal, the audience, the platforms and the creative direction before anything is shot.

  2. Shoot

    I shoot on location or in studio, thinking about the edit and the cutdowns from the first setup.

  3. Edit, grade & two revisions

    Edit and grade, then two revision rounds. Turnaround is fast and agreed per project.

  4. Delivery

    Final spots and cutdowns in the formats each platform needs.

FAQ

Good questions

How do you work out the price?

Around 5% of what the video can realistically generate for you. We agree on a sensible estimate of that value before starting, and the fee is set from it.

Which formats and aspect ratios do you deliver?

Whatever your campaign needs, for example 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5, with different lengths cut from the same shoot.

Do you travel for commercial shoots?

I work across all of Spain and can travel anywhere in Europe by arrangement. Post-production can also be done remotely for teams elsewhere.

How fast is the turnaround?

Fast turnaround is part of the service. The schedule is agreed per project so you know the delivery date before we start.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included in every project.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short brief with your goal, audience and platforms, any references you like, and the product or location if relevant. If you only have a rough idea, we shape it together on a call.
