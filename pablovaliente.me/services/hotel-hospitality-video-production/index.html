Overview

In short

Hospitality sells a feeling before it sells a room. Guests decide on atmosphere: the light at breakfast, the pool at dusk, the way a space feels when it is lived in. Good hotel video production in Spain captures that atmosphere in a film that works on your website, your social channels and with your booking partners.

I produce hospitality films for hotels and apartment complexes across Spain, and I can travel anywhere in Europe. Photography, video and drone are planned together, so one shoot gives you a consistent set of assets for the whole season.

How it works

From brief to delivery

  1. Brief

    We define the story, the guest you want to reach and the deliverables. I agree the shot list with your team.

  2. Shoot

    Shooting days planned around check-in, quiet areas and the best light, with minimal disruption to guests.

  3. Edit, grade & two revisions

    Edit and grade, then two revision rounds. Turnaround is fast and agreed per project.

  4. Delivery

    Final films, cutdowns and photos with the agreed usage licence.

FAQ

Good questions

How is a hotel quote calculated?

It is a custom quote built from shooting days, editing days, deliverables and the usage licence. Send a short brief and I will come back with a clear breakdown.

What does the usage licence cover?

It defines where and for how long you can use the finished films and photos, for example website, social media or paid advertising. It is set out in the quote.

Do you travel for hotel projects?

Yes. I work across all of Spain and can travel anywhere in Europe by arrangement.

How do you shoot in an operating hotel?

The shot list and schedule are agreed in advance and planned around check-in, quiet areas and the best light, so guests are disturbed as little as possible.

How many revisions are included, and how fast is delivery?

Two revision rounds are included in every project. Turnaround is fast and agreed per project.

Can you deliver social media cuts as well as the main film?

Yes. Vertical, square and landscape versions can be part of the deliverables from the start.
