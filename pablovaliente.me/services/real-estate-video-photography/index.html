Overview

In short

A listing is often judged in a few seconds. Property video and photography decide whether a buyer keeps scrolling or books a viewing. I shoot real estate video in Spain as one coherent package: interiors, exteriors and aerials, edited and color graded by the same person who shot them.

I work anywhere in Spain; Ibiza and Valencia are simply examples of where listings are. For properties elsewhere in Europe I can travel by arrangement. The result is a property videography package ready for the portals, social media and the agent's own website.

Who it's for

Made for people like you

For listing agents and agencies, developers and promoters, and owners of premium or distinctive properties who want a listing to look like the place feels in person.

How it works

  1. Brief

  3. Edit, grade & two revisions

    Edit, grade and retouch, with two revision rounds included. Turnaround is fast and agreed per project.

    Final video and photos in the formats and ratios your channels need.

FAQ

Good questions

Where in Spain do you work?

Ibiza and Valencia are just examples: I work anywhere in Spain and can travel across Europe by arrangement.

How is the price worked out?

Just enter the property value in the Start a project form and you get an instant estimate. The final quote is confirmed after the brief and agreed before the shoot.

Is drone footage included, and is it legal?

I hold an EASA A1/A3 drone pilot licence. Flights depend on the airspace rules and permissions at each location, which I check before the shoot.

How fast will I receive the files?

Fast turnaround is part of the service, agreed per project so you know the delivery date in advance.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included in every project.

What do you need from me before the shoot?

Access to the property, a few key facts about it, the target buyer and the channels the content will run on. Ideally the space is tidy and ready on the day.
