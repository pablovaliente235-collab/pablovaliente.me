Overview

In short

You shot the film. Now it needs a cut that holds attention and a grade that feels like one world from the first shot to the last. I work as a remote video editor and DaVinci Resolve colorist for clients worldwide, taking raw footage from a drive or a shared link and returning a finished film, ready for screens, platforms or festivals.

Because I also shoot as a director of photography, I read footage the way a cinematographer does: I know why a shot was lit the way it was and how far it can be pushed in the grade. That is why color grading services here are more than a LUT on top; shots are matched, balanced and shaped scene by scene.

What you get

Deliverables

How it works

From brief to delivery

  1. Brief

    Share the footage, references and the goal: length, platforms and audience. A short call or a written note is enough.

  2. Edit & grade

    I cut the film, lock the picture and grade it in DaVinci Resolve. Turnaround is fast and agreed per project.

  3. Two revision rounds

    You send consolidated notes; I apply them. Two rounds are included.

  4. Delivery

    Final masters and versions in the formats you need.

FAQ

Good questions

How fast can you turn a project around?

Fast turnaround is part of the service. The schedule is agreed per project once I have seen the footage and the brief, so you know the delivery date before we start.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included in every project. Collect your notes in one place per round and I will apply them together.

How is the price calculated?

The price depends on the number and length of pieces, the footage, deliverables and formats, color space and revisions. Two revision rounds are included and each extra round is added on top. You get an instant estimate with your brief.

What do I need to send you?

The footage or proxies with a link to the originals, any reference films or looks, music or voice-over if you have them, and a short note on audience, platform and length.

Which software and color workflows do you use?

I edit and grade in DaVinci Resolve and I am a Blackmagic Design certified editor and colorist. I work in DCI-P3 or Rec.709 depending on where the film will be shown.

Can you work with clients outside Spain?

Yes. I am based in Spain with bases in Germany and the USA, and remote post-production is available worldwide, so time zones are rarely a problem.

Which formats and aspect ratios can you deliver?

Whatever your platforms need, for example 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5. Tell me the destinations in the brief and I will plan the versions from the start.
