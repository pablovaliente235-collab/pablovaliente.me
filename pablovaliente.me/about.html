About me

Spain-based Audiovisual Professional, DaVinci Resolve-certified Editor, Colorist, and EASA licensed drone pilot. I provide end-to-end visual solutions, bridging cinematic narrative and premium commercial content to translate spaces, architectures, and ideas into high-fidelity moving images.

With a degree in Photography and Audiovisual Creation from EASD Valencia, my approach to production is meticulous. From managing pre-production, scouting, and permits to delivering the final frame, I ensure seamless execution, precise pacing, and rigorous color grading across DCI-P3 and Rec.709 standards.

Whether post-producing independent short films as a specialized editor and colorist, upgrading premium brand content, or executing full-scale media productions for the real estate and hospitality sectors — my goal is to elevate raw material into a flawless, high-value cinematic experience.
